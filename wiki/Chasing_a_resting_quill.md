# _Chasing a resting quill_
The expression "to chase a resting quill" is a metaphor/allegory for ensuring a fanfiction is "as canon as possible" even if the main plot has already been privately written by the original author.

I coined the phrase in the _"FATE: Festival"_ disbloat guild, while we were discussing what to do after Deltarune ch3+4 released:
> I have a crazy idea: what if we shift the branching-point? That is, instead of this AU deviating from the main story at the end of chapter 2, what if we deviate at ch1? (maybe ch0??)
>
> We could still use all of what we learned from the main plot, and the assets (both main/prime and FATE), it's just that the plot would end up being much more different than originally intended. And there's no need to rewrite ch 1 & 2, we can just skip those, as if they're the ch -1 & 0 of this AU
>
> I'm saying this because now I believe it's hopeless to "chase a resting quill" (I made up that quote rn)
